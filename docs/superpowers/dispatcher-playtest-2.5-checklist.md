# Dispatcher — playtest 2.5 checklist: platform overflow

A short playtest of the crowding work only (F29 and F30 in
`dispatcher-playtest-2-feedback.md`). Write what you see and feel next to each
box; numbers you would change are the most useful notes.

## Setup

- [ ] `main` (merged at e799a63), `npm run dev`. The hint lives in the dev
      build only; production is Off.
- [ ] The dev panel is bottom left. Click the row "Crowding: B · 5 s" to open
      it: Crowding hint (Off / A / B), Hint delay (0–15 s), Declutter labels.
- [ ] To meet the new tip again later, press How to play on the ready screen
      (it clears the seen tips).
- [ ] Quickest way to see crowding: let Shift 1 run into the Evening peak
      (about 55 s in). KLCC and Masjid Jamek fill first.

Known and left alone: the dev panel covers the first letters of the typing
prompt ("Typ") at 1280 × 800.

## 1. The first time it shows (the tip)

- [ ] The Career pauses on the crowding tip the first time a ring or an amber
      chip appears. Was the moment right, or did it interrupt something?
- [ ] The camera zooms to the crowded Station and the amber Trains pulse. Did
      you see which Station and which Trains it meant?
- [ ] After pressing Enter, did you know what to type?

## 2. Mode B: amber chips and a ring

- [ ] Can you read the ring and the waiting count without stopping?
- [ ] The crowded Station keeps its name beside its ring. Always?
- [ ] When a Train calls and the crowd drops below 55, the ring vanishes at
      once (today's rule). Clear, or does it feel like a glitch?
- [ ] Shift 8 or later: do several rings clutter the centre?
- [ ] A locked amber chip (you are typing it) still reads as locked?

## 3. Mode A: amber chips only

- [ ] Without a ring, do the amber chips alone tell you enough?
- [ ] A or B: which tells you more for less clutter? Which should players get?

## 4. Hint delay

- [ ] Try 0 s, 5 s and 10 s. Does the hint come too late to act, or so often
      it becomes noise? (The probe: at 5 s some ring shows 31 % of the time in
      Shifts 1–3, 50 % from Shift 9.)
- [ ] Value that felt right: ______ s

## 5. Declutter labels

- [ ] With it on, only Incident places and ringed Stations keep their names;
      Held Trains are named by their Signal chips. Is the map calmer?
- [ ] Did you lose your bearings without termini and Interchange names?
- [ ] Between Shifts the Expansion offers keep their names. Correct?
- [ ] Keep it, drop it, or keep some names (which)?

## 5b. Amber rule and the grey ring

- [ ] Dev panel, Amber rule: "Arrives in time" (default) or "Within 3 blocks".
- [ ] With Arrives in time: did any amber Train you Cleared quickly still fail
      to save its Station? Note the Station and how far back the Train was.
- [ ] A grey ring: did you understand that Station was lost? Did the grey-ring
      tip make sense?
- [ ] Did a grey ring help answer "how did that happen?" after the closure?
- [ ] Too many grey rings, so the map feels hopeless? Which Shift?
- [ ] Arrives in time or Within 3 blocks: which one should players get?

## 6. Did it change how you play?

- [ ] Did you Clear amber Trains first when you saw them?
- [ ] After a crowding closure, did you know how you could have avoided it?
- [ ] Any closure you could not have prevented whatever you typed? (Expected:
      Titiwangsa or PWTC in Shift 2, when their new Line has one or two Trains.)

- [ ] Zoom (6× in Shift 1, at least 4× later): too tight, right, too loose?
- [ ] With reduced motion on, the camera stays still. Fine?
